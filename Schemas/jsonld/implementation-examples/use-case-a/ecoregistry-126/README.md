# Example 1: Karapınar YEKA Solar Power Plant Project

Country: Turkey
Registry: EcoRegistry
Methodology: CDM - ACM0002: Grid-connected electricity generation from renewable sources
URL: https://www.ecoregistry.io/projects/126

## Overview

"The Karapınar YEKA Solar Power Plant Project... is designed as a greenfield solar photovoltaic power plant and the produced energy will be fed into the Turkish national grid... It reduces the air pollution caused by the grid connected power plants which are mostly fossil fuel fired." (Project Description Document, pp. 4-5.) The project includes several secondary activities aimed at benefiting households in and around the project area.

# Environments

Although no environment of primary concern is explicitly identified in the project documentation, the intended/implied environment of primary concern is the atmosphere of Earth extending from sea level to 6 km outwards (i.e., that portion of Earth's atmosphere which is affected by GHGs).

Several other environments of concern can be identified for the project's secondary activities. For the Social Development Program, for example, the environment is identified as "households engaged in animal husbandry or agriculture in Büyükkarakuyu, Küçükkarakuyu, Kirkitoğlu, Ekmekçi, Seyit Hacı villages."

## State differences

The state difference of primary concern in this example is that between (a) the baseline state in which the Karapinar YEKA SPPP does not exist and Turkey's electricity is mainly generated from fossil fuels, and (b) the project state in which the Karapinar YEKA SPPP exists and Turkey's electricity is consequently at least partially generated from solar power.
